## 📖 About the Book
This repository contains the structural framework and selected previews from the upcoming book **"Prescriptive AI"**. 

The book serves as a definitive guide for healthcare professionals, clinical pharmacists, and AI trainers, focusing on the safe, ethical, and highly effective integration of Large Language Models (LLMs) into daily clinical workflows.

---

## 📑 Core Chapters & Framework Sneak Peek

### 🔹 1. Advanced Clinical Prompt Engineering
- Moving beyond basic prompts to structured, constraint-based querying.
- Building fail-safes into prompts to ensure zero-hallucination outputs in critical medical scenarios.
- Tailoring LLM persona configurations for clinical decision support.

### 🔹 2. Visual Data Extraction for Handwritten Prescriptions
- Utilizing Multimodal LLMs (Vision-Language Models) to decode and digitize complex, handwritten medical prescriptions.
- Structuring the extracted data into standardized JSON formats for pharmacy management systems.
- Error mitigation and confidence scoring in handwriting recognition.

### 🔹 3. Back-Translation Workflows in Healthcare
- Ensuring absolute accuracy in medical translation using automated back-translation loops.
- Validating patient instructions and localized medical leaflets across multiple languages without losing clinical context.
- Cross-referencing translated medical terminologies with standard databases.
